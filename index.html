Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Jansher Bloch, operating as Desinyo Tech ("we", "us"), handles information in the Android game Catch the Starship, package com.desinyo.catchthestarship.

For privacy questions or requests, contact desinyotech@gmail.com.

At a glance

  • Progress and settings are saved on your device.
  • Optional usage analytics is off by default and offered only with an adult (18+) agreement.

Information saved on your device

The game stores scores, clean and assisted records, older records, stage progress, rewards, appearance choices, saved flights, control and audio settings, and privacy preferences in its private Android storage. We use this information to operate the game and restore your progress and choices.

The game does not ask for your name, email address, contact list, date of birth or precise GPS location. It does not require an account or provide account-based cloud recovery. Clearing app data or uninstalling can erase your saved progress. Advertisements and optional analytics require an internet connection; core gameplay and Restart do not.

Optional usage analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand gameplay and improve the game. Optional analytics is off by default. It is enabled only when a player chooses the in-game agreement stating that they are 18 or older and agree to optional usage analytics. This declaration is not verified age identification.

With this option enabled, events can include runs, scores, completed stages, active play duration, failures, restarts, revival-ad outcomes and selected ship or background categories. Google can also process app and device information, pseudonymous app-instance identifiers, session information and approximate geographic information derived from network information. These records are not necessarily anonymous.

Our custom gameplay events do not include your name, email address, birthday, contact list, precise location or raw control input. Firebase Analytics advertising-ID collection is disabled in the app. The analytics choice is separate from advertising privacy choices.

You can play without optional analytics. Turning it off does not change game difficulty, controls or rewards. In Settings → Privacy & advertising, use Turn off & reset analytics to disable collection and reset local analytics data and the app-instance identifier. This does not automatically delete information previously uploaded to Google or existing aggregated reports.

Advertising and privacy choices

The app uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded revivals, limited to three successful revivals per run, and banners on menu or results screens. Restart does not require viewing an ad. We do not show forced interstitial or app-open ads.

Google's advertising services can process network information such as your IP address, approximate location, app and ad interactions, diagnostic or performance information, and device or other identifiers. This supports ad delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention. Non-personalized ads can still use information for these purposes.

The app requests non-personalized advertising and applies teen-oriented advertising restrictions. Its Android advertising-ID permission is removed; however, this is not a promise that all advertising identifiers or other identifiers are absent on every supported Android version.

Google's User Messaging Platform is used to request advertising consent or provide privacy options where applicable, including relevant European and US regional messages when configured. The message shown depends on your region and the service configuration. Where available, revisit Advertising privacy choices in the game's privacy settings. Ad availability depends on your choices, connectivity and available inventory.

Where consent is required, it is the basis for the relevant optional processing. We use information needed to operate the game, respond to requests, maintain security and meet applicable legal obligations for those purposes. A consent choice for advertising does not enable the separate optional usage analytics setting.

Service providers, sharing and international processing

Google provides the Firebase Analytics, AdMob and consent-management services used by the app. Information handled by these services may be processed by Google and its service partners, including advertising partners disclosed in the applicable consent message. Google's role and handling of information depend on the particular service, applicable terms, your region and your choices.

These providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States and other countries where they operate. See their privacy and service information for the entities involved, processing locations and applicable transfer safeguards:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase privacy and security information
  • How Google uses information from apps and sites that use its services

When you choose Share My Best, the game creates a score-card image and opens Android's sharing chooser. You decide whether to share and which recipient or service to use. The game does not automatically publish your score to social media. The service you choose has its own privacy practices.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the content you send. We use this information to answer your question, handle your request and keep necessary support records. Please do not send passwords, verification codes or unnecessary sensitive information.

How long information is retained

  • Local game information: retained on your device until removed through available game or Android controls, or until app data is cleared. Uninstalling can remove local progress.
  • Firebase Analytics event data: our current event-data retention setting is 2 months.
  • Firebase Analytics user data: our current user-data retention setting is 14 months, with reset on new user activity enabled. New activity can extend the retention of the user identifier; this is not a fixed 14-month limit from first use.
  • Aggregated analytics: most standard aggregated reports are not subject to those user/event retention limits and may remain available longer. Google's deletion processing follows its service schedules.
  • Advertising and consent information: handled under the relevant Google service retention practices, legal requirements and privacy choices. The Firebase retention settings above do not set a retention period for all AdMob information.
  • Support correspondence: retained for as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request and maintain necessary support, security or legal records.

See Google Analytics data retention information for how these settings and deletion schedules work.

Teenagers and younger players

The game is intended for teenagers and adults and is not directed to children under 13. Content suitability ratings are separate from this intended audience. Optional usage analytics is offered only with the 18+ agreement described above; we do not implement verified age identification or a parental-consent service.

If you are below the age at which you can make the relevant privacy choices under your local law, ask a parent or guardian for guidance. Do not enable optional usage analytics if you are under 18. If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information to us or improperly enabled optional analytics, contact us so that we can review the circumstances and available deletion steps.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can turn optional analytics off and reset its local data, revisit advertising privacy choices where available, manage app permissions in Android, and remove local game information by clearing app data. Clearing data also removes saved progress. There is no account to delete.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or portability of personal information, object to or restrict certain processing, withdraw consent, or complain to your local data-protection authority. Withdrawing consent does not make earlier processing unlawful.

Send requests to desinyotech@gmail.com with the app name and a description of your request. We may need limited information to verify and locate relevant records. Because gameplay analytics uses pseudonymous identifiers rather than a player account, we may be unable to associate an email address with particular analytics records. We will explain the options available and use provider deletion tools where applicable; we cannot promise to identify or remove every record from aggregated reports.

Security

Local game information is held in the app's private Android storage, and Google SDK network communications use transport encryption. These measures reduce risk, but no storage or transmission system is completely secure.
